Where it stands

Four milestones are done. The rp2350 package (0.2.0) now covers both QFN-60 and QFN-80 on 2 and 4 layers, and the crystal, USB, flash and decoupling, mined from Raspberry Pi's design guide and minimal boards. Earlier: Intent schema v0 and its validator pass easy2350. A buck generator, fast-tier checks and the first package, packages/rp2350 0.1.0, cover the RP2350's core regulator. Starting from nothing but the part footprints, the generator places CIN and COUT where Raspberry Pi did. Every row in the table below is measured by the same checker, and the generated layout comes out ahead on all four checks. It is KiCad DRC clean on a 4-layer test bench. The compiled routing.json now drives ultraroute through --regions, and easy2350 routed that way passes the seam check on three seeds.
