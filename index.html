circuit-composer

Milestone 3 in progress

Board design as composition: chips ingested once into verified packages, boards built by combining them. Sits between easy_pcb (schematic) and ultraroute (routing).

Where it stands

Two milestones are done. Intent schema v0 and its validator pass easy2350. A buck generator, fast-tier checks and the first package, packages/rp2350 0.1.0, cover the RP2350's core regulator. Starting from nothing but the part footprints, the generator places CIN and COUT where Raspberry Pi did. Every row in the table below is measured by the same checker, and the generated layout comes out ahead on all four checks. It is KiCad DRC clean on a 4-layer test bench. Nothing is routed by ultraroute yet; that is milestone 3.
